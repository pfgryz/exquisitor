Article Type:

Scientific article in area measuring and monitoring sustainability

Title:

Accelerating Metagenomic Identification of DNA Sequences Using Artificial Neural Networks

Abstract:

The growing volume of DNA sequence data demands efficient metagenomic
identification. It provides the possibility to construct the tools to
monitor enviromental conditions, risks related to organisms and pathogens
and sustainability performance. We present Exquisitor, an ANN-based tool
leveraging contrastive learning
to select representative sequences, improving computational efficiency.
Benchmarking against classical methods shows superior classification
accuracy and competitive execution time, highlighting ANNs
scalability and precision for genetic taxonomy.

Keywords: artificial neural networks, contrastive learning, DNA
sequencing, metagenomic identification, sequence clustering, monitor
enviromental conditions, sustainability
